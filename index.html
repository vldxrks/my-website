MEETING-ROOM BOOKING 01 / 04
01 — BOOKING & BUSINESS LOGICNEW PROJECT

SlotFlow

A meeting-room booking service with a 15-minute slot hold. Find a room, choose a time and confirm your booking, with a separate dashboard for administrators.

  • Python
  • Flask
  • SQLite
  • Vanilla JavaScript
Under the hood +

A partial unique database index prevents duplicate active bookings for the same room, date and fixed slot. Expired holds are released during booking and availability requests. Includes session authentication, owner checks, admin-only endpoints and smoke tests for the main booking flow.

Built as a focused prototype with fixed time slots. Pricing is calculated per booking; payments and email notifications are not implemented.
